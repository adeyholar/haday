# HaDay → HebRabbAI plan

Four slices. Each one ships to classmates on its own. Do them **in order** unless Crown picks a later one first.

Not in this plan: live Hebrew ASR scoring, commentary RAG, peer rooms, native apps, offline pack. Those wait until 1–4 are in use.

Tick the status when a slice ships (`queued` → `building` → `shipped`).

---

## 1. Tap a word · lexicon card — `queued`

**Classmates get.** In follow-along, tap any Hebrew word. A card opens: the form, class gloss if it is a BBH lemma, OSHB tags (noun/verb, binyan, person/gender/number, construct), and a button to deal that grammar in Tanakh cards or jump to Listen on that verse.

**We will not.** Guess a shoresh we do not have. No etymology essay. No TTS of a made-up citation form.

**Done when.** iPad: tap word → card; tap outside closes; highlighted spoken word still works. Works in Torah first, then the rest of the Tanakh.

**Builds on.** Kaldi highlight, OSHB query index, Tanakh cards.

---

## 2. Echo the verse · ears first — `queued`

**Classmates get.** After a verse (or a chosen range), **Echo**: the recording plays, then the mic opens. Student reads. Both takes play back, Hebrew still on screen, spoken word still marked. Optional: save the take on the account for the owner to hear later.

**We will not.** Auto-grade pronunciation. No red/green phoneme score (that would lie).

**Done when.** One verse echo on iPad without a second seek bar. Stop/re-record is obvious. No take is stored unless the student keeps it.

**Builds on.** Follow-along player, Voice bank recorder.

---

## 3. Ask HaDay on this verse — `queued`

**Classmates get.** From the word card or the verse, **Ask about this**. The tutor already knows book, chapter, verse, the Hebrew line, the tapped form, and class vocab in that verse. Answers stay on the lesson and the open text.

**We will not.** Pull Rashi, Talmud, or copyrighted BBH pages. No vector store yet.

**Done when.** A question like “why is this qamets hatuf?” names the open word and the chain, not a generic textbook paragraph.

**Builds on.** Slice 1 (the card knows the form) + Ask HaDay.

---

## 4. Pronunciation later · only with a trusted ear — `queued`

**Classmates get.** Only after 2 is in daily use. Either (a) owner/teacher marks echo takes, or (b) a Hebrew ASR we have actually tested on class audio. Then: one simple score plus **what to try again** (vowel, shewa, stress) — never a fake 97%.

**We will not.** Ship a score that beginners cannot trust.

**Done when.** A recorded echo can be reviewed, and any machine score is labeled as a hint, not a grade.

**Builds on.** Slice 2 + a chosen ASR or teacher workflow.
